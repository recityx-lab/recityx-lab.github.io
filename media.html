Yan Wang, Ph.D.

Associate Professor, Department of Urban and Regional Planning, University of Florida · Founder and Director, ReCityX Lab

Dr. Yan Wang is an Associate Professor with Tenure in the Department of Urban & Regional Planning at the University of Florida. Her research uses big data, computation, and AI to understand and strengthen how cities and communities withstand, recover from, and adapt to a range of disruptions, from hazards to emerging technologies. She is a recipient of the NSF CAREER Award (2025).
