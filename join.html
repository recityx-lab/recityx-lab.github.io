Join Us

If you are a self-motivated, creative researcher who wants to work across planning, engineering, data science, and AI, we hope you will apply to join the ReCityX Lab. We look for team members who enjoy hard problems and want their research to matter for people and places facing disruption. Our PhD and postdoc alumni now work as faculty and researchers. Visit the Team page to learn more.

Current Openings

PhD Students

We are recruiting PhD students in Urban and Regional Planning. Successful candidates will have strong interest in one of the lab’s research areas:

  • AI and future-ready city planning
  • Urban resilience, disaster response, and community health

Candidates should hold a bachelor’s or master’s degree in planning, engineering, geography, computer science, or a related analytical field, with a strong background in spatial data analysis and/or machine learning. Programming skills (e.g., Python) and experience with GIS are strongly preferred. Candidates must also apply to the University of Florida to be considered.

Postdoctoral Scholar

A postdoctoral position is available to conduct research on urban resilience, spatial planning, and data-driven analysis of how communities and built environments respond to disruptions. Candidates should hold a Ph.D. in planning, engineering, geography, computer science, or a related analytical field, with a strong background in spatial data analysis and/or machine learning. Strong candidates will have a record of publishing in peer-reviewed journals.

NSF REU Program (NSF Funded)

A 6-month to 1-year research program for undergraduate students who are U.S. citizens or permanent residents. Students will work on GenAI and resilient spatial planning.

To apply

Email your CV, a summary of your research skills, your goals, and the names of 2 references. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis until positions are filled.

How to Express Interest

Email yanw@ufl.edu using the exact subject line for your position. Attach your CV and a short statement of research interests as a single PDF named LastName_FirstName_Position_DesiredStartingSemester.pdf (for example, Smith_Jane_PhD_Fall2027.pdf). We receive many inquiries and cannot respond to every message. Emails without the correct subject line may not be reviewed.

Other inquiries, including from visiting scholars, can use the subject line [ReCityX-Inquiry] Your Full Name | Topic.
