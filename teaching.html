Courses

URP 6223

Introduction to Urban Analytics

Graduate · 3 credits · On campus (Fall) and online (Spring)

Core course for the Urban Analytics master’s and certificate program. Integrates urban science theory with Python-based data analytics across modules on sensing the city, urban data infrastructure, visualizing the city, differences within cities, explanatory urban models, cities as networks, ethics, and generative urban systems.

URP 6270

Introduction to Planning Information System

Graduate · 3 credits · Fall and Spring

Fully redeveloped curriculum and lab sessions using ArcGIS Pro. Serves as the foundational GIS course for multiple programs, including the MURP and MUA.

URP 4273

Surveying Planning Information System

Undergraduate · 3 credits · Fall

Survey to Planning Information Systems is intended to introduce undergraduate students to the concepts, principles, and reality of using Geographic Information Systems (GIS). It teaches the essential skills of operating a functional GIS mainly using ArcGIS Pro. This powerful technology provides users with a very effective tool for capturing, analyzing, and displaying spatial data that is crucial to the planning and decision-making process. The course is both theoretical and practical (i.e., very hands-on), addressing both the structure of geographic information systems and the use of this tool within planning for spatial analysis and data management. Our applications also address a variety of planning topics such as resilience, sustainability, and accessibility, among others.
